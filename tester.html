iPhone Wheel IMU Tester

Return-to-zero test for evaluating iPhone motion-sensor drift before building a wheel-alignment workflow.
Open this page in Safari over HTTPS. Tap Enable Motion Sensors, place the phone in a rigid repeatable fixture, wait for STATIONARY, tap ZERO, move the phone for 10–30 s, then return it to the exact same fixture and tap SAVE TRIAL.
